# @operai/letterhead-api

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 1e948ef: The look of the document title is now adjustable in Letterheads. The look section of the configurator has a new "Document title" group next to the three heading levels, with size (8 to 36 pt), bold and colour. It styles the title line of a document, such as the "CONTRATTO DI FORNITURA" line of a CRM offer, which is always set in the headings font. The default is the 20 pt bold black used until now, so a version saved earlier opens unchanged and prints exactly as it did; a change of the title is listed as a change of "Headings and document title" in the version history.
- 856635d: Letterheads can now be set in Inter, and the distance between the header or footer and the body is adjustable. Inter (regular, bold and italic) joins the font list for the body text, the headings and the header and footer. Each header and footer has a new "Distance from the body (mm)" field, between 0 and 40 mm, 3 mm by default: increase it to move the logo and header text further from the body. A distance that no longer fits the margin together with the header or footer is refused on save as before ("does not fit the top margin"), and a version saved earlier opens with 3 mm and prints exactly as it did.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 8048f95: Rendered documents now print the issuer's legal name, address, VAT number, IBAN and contacts from the legal entities registry in auth, in the document's language, replacing any company details sent with the request. Content version 2 is new: it carries an optional `legalEntityVersion` pin and no company details; version 1 still works, with its company details ignored in favour of the registry. An empty value in the registry prints as a missing placeholder. When the registry cannot be read the render answers 503 `legal_entities_unavailable` with `Retry-After: 5` and makes no job. A pin that differs from the registry's version answers 409 `legal_entity_version_mismatch` with `currentVersion`; a pin older than or equal to the held version is refused at once without reading the registry, a newer pin refreshes it at most once a second and at most 100 times in 10 minutes per process, and beyond that the 409 comes straight away. The answers carry the version used (`legalEntityVersion` in the preview JSON, `Legal-Entity-Version` on the PDF). The configurator preview shows the entity's real details in the preview language, an empty value drawn as its label in a distinct look and as ⟦Label⟧ in the page text; the answer gains `issuerDetails` (`current` or `unavailable`), and an unreadable registry still gives a preview with every detail marked. The sample document's intro is reworded for real company details.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 4b6e43e: Add letterhead-api (specs/20261116-letterhead-service, ADR 20261116-letterhead-api-is-a-rust-service-with-typst-embedded-for-document-layout-only): the one Rust service in the monorepo, a Cargo workspace (`engine`, a pure crate with Typst embedded for layout only, and `api`, an axum supervisor). It renders a document's pages and PDF from a content model and a versioned letterhead configuration (`POST /v1/render/preview`, `/v1/render/pdf`, `GET /v1/letterheads/status`, called by service clients holding the `letterhead:render` scope), and serves the Letterheads tool: the catalog, the list, versions, history, asset upload, save, restore and a sample-document preview (`/v1/catalog`, `/v1/letterheads/...`), with person auth and entity-scoped permissions (the scope table is shared with crm-api through `docs/fixtures/entity-scope.json`). A letterhead version pins its layout, and old layouts stay compiled in (golden corpus and pdfium conformance in CI). Renders run in a killable worker pool bounded by memory (2 GiB per worker by default), a document over the page limit is refused 422 `too_many_pages`, and nothing of a document is kept (residue test). Assets are sniffed, bounded and, for SVG, rebuilt. Postgres holds the letterheads with immutability triggers and a change record; S3 holds the assets under the EU-residency guard ported from crm-api's `s3Residency.ts`. Wired into the monorepo by hand: a pinned mise `rust` toolchain and a `dev:letterhead` task, a compose service with its database and bucket, a multi-stage non-root Docker image, its Railway declaration, this Changesets shim, the changeset-check path filter, the env-doctor manifest and production template, and a CI workflow that runs fmt, clippy, tests, the golden corpus, the worst-case table, the conformance run and an advisory scan of Cargo.lock.
