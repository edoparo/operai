# specs/20261101-crm-sales-flow — decisions

On 2026-11-01 the product owner approved the spec and chose the four directions in its
header. They did not answer the open questions O1–O4, so the agent decided them here,
together with the details the build required. Each entry gives what was decided, why, and
what was rejected. The architecturally significant ones are ADR 20261101-crm-financial-visibility-permission and ADR 20261101-app-access-check-by-user-id-and-directory-app-filter.

| # | Decision | Why | Rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 | **O1 — the offer scope prints releases, then epics, with man-days** (Expected = PERT + risk; AI-assisted on request). There is no activity list, no profile breakdown and no schedule. | An offer quotes the effort the client buys, at a level the client can read. Activities are internal working detail, and the elapsed/FTE chain is a staffing plan, not a commitment. | Every activity (too long, and it exposes the internal breakdown); a single total (too little to discuss). |
| D-2 | **O1/O2 — letterhead and standard terms are a CRM setting per entity** (`entity_profile`: legal name, up to 5 address lines, VAT number, IBAN, terms in EN and IT), edited on a new Settings tab by `deal:manage` holders of that entity. An offer refuses to generate (409 `entity_profile_missing`) until the profile exists. | No existing source in the suite holds the companies' own legal data. The people who send offers are the ones who know the terms. The offer snapshots what it printed, so later edits never rewrite a sent offer. | Environment variables (a deploy to change an address); hard-coded text (no Italian/English per entity); an admin-only screen (the admin is not the one writing terms). |
| D-3 | **O3 — deal notes are hidden from callers without `deal:financials`**, like amounts. | Notes are where prices and discounts get written ("10% agreed"). Hiding them costs the technician little; leaking them defeats US-1. | Showing notes (leaks prices); a separate "technical notes" field (a model change nobody asked for; the activity timeline already serves that purpose). |
| D-4 | **O4 — `crm-technical` does not imply EstimAI access.** The admin grants both roles. A technician without EstimAI is refused at request time with 422 `technician_not_estimai_user`, and the error tells the salesperson to ask an admin. | Roles stay one bundle per app (specs/20260713-auth-roles-permissions). Coupling two apps' access in one role would make revoking either one surprising. | A combined role; granting EstimAI automatically from crm-api (crm-api never writes grants). |
| D-5 | **The estimate is created as the salesperson, with their forwarded JWT.** The salesperson owns it and the technician is an `editor` (specs/20260807-estimate-sharing). crm-api finds the technician's email in auth's directory filtered by `appId=estimai`, which the caller can open because they hold `deal:manage` (ADR 20261101-user-directory-gate-widened-to-deal-manage). | No new trust between services and no service credential for estimai-api. EstimAI keeps enforcing its own ACL. The salesperson can follow the estimate. | A service credential for crm-api (a new secret, and an estimate owned by nobody); the technician as owner (the requester would lose sight of it). |
| D-6 | **If the collaborator grant fails, the new estimate is deleted again** (best effort), and nothing is linked. | A half-made estimate that nobody was asked to fill is clutter in the requester's EstimAI list. | Keeping it and linking it (the technician could not open it). |
| D-7 | **Gate rule: a move FROM `lead`, `qualified` or `lost` TO `proposal`, `negotiation` or `won` needs the estimate `ready` or an admin override (409 `estimate_not_ready`).** Moves among proposal, negotiation and won are never gated. A new deal starts only as `lead` or `qualified` (422 `stage_requires_estimate`). | `lost` is in the "from" list so lead → lost → won cannot step around the gate. Creating straight into proposal would be the same hole. Deals already past qualified when this ships are never re-gated. | Gating only from qualified (both holes stay open); gating every move into proposal (would block a legacy deal moving proposal ↔ negotiation). |
| D-8 | **"Admin" means `deal:manage` over every entity** (`managesAllDeals`). An admin overrides the gate, marks ready or reopens on anyone's behalf, and deletes anyone's offer or attachment. | It is the reach the `admin` role already has, it is visible in `GET /scope` as `managesAll`, and it needs no new permission. | A new `crm:admin` permission (no reason to separate it yet); "holds `contact:erase`" (an unrelated privacy capability). |
| D-9 | **An override is recorded on the `deal_estimate` row and as a note on the deal's timeline.** It works with or without a linked estimate, and survives unlinking it. | AC-3.7 asks for the reason to be visible. The timeline is where people look, and the row is what the gate reads. | A separate audit table (Tier 2 with no reader); an override that a re-request erases. |
| D-10 | **The technician's task is created with the request**: subject "Estimate: <deal>", due 5 working days later (Mon–Fri, no holidays). Marking the estimate ready completes it; reopening the estimate reopens it. | The task list is where technicians look (US-4 of specs/20261101-crm). Keeping the two states in step avoids a finished task for an unfinished estimate. | No task (the request would be invisible); a notification (still specs/20261101-crm D-14). |
| D-11 | **Offer numbering is per deal, `v1, v2, …`, under an advisory lock.** The reference is `OF-<year>-<deal id tail>-<n>`. A deleted version keeps its number: the gap shows, and the number is never reused. | Two generators cannot collide. A sent document's reference must stay unique forever. | A global sequence (meaningless to the client); reusing numbers. |
| D-12 | **The offer PDF is rendered by crm-api with `pdf-lib` and standard Helvetica**, from a snapshot stored on the row: letterhead, client, scope figures, price, terms, author. Characters outside WinAnsi print as "?". | The same choice as ADR 20260719-refund-batch-pdf-generation-pdf-lib, with no browser dependency. The snapshot keeps exactly what was sent, even if the estimate changes later. | A headless browser; re-rendering from live data (the client's copy and ours would differ); embedding a TTF font now (a follow-up if names need it). |
| D-13 | **The price defaults to the deal amount and can be changed per offer.** Validity defaults to 30 days (1–365). Terms default to the entity's standard terms in the offer's language. | It matches how offers are negotiated: a revised price is a new version, while the deal's amount stays the pipeline figure. | Locking the price to the deal amount (each revision would edit the deal). |
| D-14 | **Attachments are two-phase (pending → stored), at most 20 MiB, in five types:** PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPEG. A technician downloads only their own uploads. Delete is the uploader's or an admin's (soft delete plus object removal). | This is inventory-api's proven flow (ADR 20260716-eu-object-storage-refund-attachments posture). The type list covers offers, specifications and screenshots. The download rule follows from US-1: an attachment may be a signed offer. | Proxying bytes through crm-api; letting anyone delete any file. |
| D-15 | **`ESTIMAI_API_URL` and `CRM_S3_*` are optional in crm-api's env.ts (all-or-none for S3, EU residency checked when set).** Without them the estimate and file routes answer 503 (`estimai_unconfigured` / `storage_unconfigured`) and the rest of the CRM works. env-doctor checks them when present but does not require them yet. | A developer's local setup and the not-yet-provisioned production (specs/20261101-crm T16) keep starting. The 503 code tells the UI exactly what is missing. | Required variables (the service would not start until the infrastructure exists). |
| D-16 | **The agent did not edit the env files themselves** (`crm-api/.env.example`, the env-doctor production template `crm-api.env`, the developer's `.env`). | The repository's secret-file guard blocks the agent from writing any `.env*` file. The lines to add are in `crm-api/README.md` and in tasks.md T9. | Working around the guard. |
