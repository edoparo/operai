# @operai/meetings-api

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 04e83a0: Personal meeting folders: each person can create folders, file each meeting they see in one of them, and filter the meetings list by folder (or by "Not in a folder"). Folders are private to their owner (ADR 20261113-personal-meeting-folders).
- 49aa7ec: Meetings get an agenda: an ordered list of points (a title and an optional note, up to 30) that the creator and the Operai participants with Meetings access write, reorder and tick as covered, also from the recorder widget during a recording; everyone who can open the meeting reads it. A summary can follow it: the new built-in template "By agenda" has one section per point plus "Other topics", and any other template can "Follow the agenda". The agenda is quoted data to the AI, never instructions, and a summary keeps the agenda it was made from.
- 6f49453: A possible gap in a transcript is now marked only when at least 60 seconds of sound have no words (was 20), and the creator can mark a range "No speech here" after listening to it: the warning disappears from the transcript, and summaries and answers stop saying the transcript may be incomplete because of it.
- ac0103a: Meetings are transcribed by Gemini on Vertex AI (EU): a meeting up to 30 minutes goes in one request, so the same person keeps one speaker label for the whole meeting instead of a new one every 10 minutes. The reply is accepted only in the expected structure, request logging for the model is checked off at startup, and Chirp 3 stays available with `MEETINGS_TRANSCRIBER=chirp`.
- c30a110: Meetings moves its AI providers to Google Cloud, EU only (specs/20261104-meetings amendment 2026-11-05, ADR 20261105-meetings-ai-providers-google-cloud-eu). Transcription is Cloud Speech-to-Text v2 Chirp 3 batch through a transient EU bucket, and summaries and Q&A are Claude on Vertex AI in the EU, replacing Azure Speech and Bedrock. Transcripts now keep their parts (with per-part speakers) and the transcript endpoint returns them. The consent notice moves to version 2026-11-05 and discloses transcript retention. Deleting a meeting or its recording also empties the transient transcription prefix first.
  - **Operators:** meetings-api needs the Google project, location and a Workload Identity Federation credential file, plus the transient bucket (`infra/meetings-gcs-transient-policy.sh --check`). It refuses to start when a startup check fails (wrong region, bucket lifecycle, soft delete, retention or caching settings). The Azure and Bedrock variables are gone; `tools/env-doctor` (not a versioned package) carries the new contract.
  - **meetings-ui:** shows transcript parts and the updated consent text.
- 4e06a46: Personal meeting notes: everyone who can open a meeting has their own notes on it, private to them (not the meeting's creator, not the AI, not the logs), written in a WYSIWYG editor (bold, italic, a heading, bulleted, numbered and check lists) and saved by themselves a moment after typing stops, when leaving the editor or the page; a save from a stale tab asks to reload instead of overwriting. The design system gains `RichTextEditor` in its own entry, `@operai/ui/rich-text` (TipTap, installed only by the apps that use it), and six formatting icons.
- c30a110: Add Meetings (specs/20261104-meetings, ADR 20261104-meetings-tool-shape-visibility-by-participation to ADR 20261104-default-employee-grant-meetings-access): record, transcribe, summarise and question meetings from anywhere in Operai. New services `meetings-api` and `meetings-ui`.
  - **Capture:** record in the browser (microphone, plus the audio of a shared tab where the browser allows it), upload a recording, or paste text. The person who records attests consent, and that attestation is stored on the meeting.
  - **Transcripts and summaries:** meetings are transcribed in Italian or English, summarised from shared or personal templates, and can be questioned one at a time, with answers citing transcript passages. Questions and answers are private to the asker. Audio is kept for 30 days; transcripts and summaries live until the meeting is deleted. AI providers run in the EU with zero retention.
  - **Visibility:** a meeting is visible to its creator and its Operai participants. The creator can optionally open it to everyone who can open the host record. A meeting whose creator is deactivated is frozen, not orphaned.
  - **auth:** new `meetings` catalog (`meetings:access`, `template:manage`, `meeting:erase`). `employee` now holds `meetings:access` by default, the first default grant on that role, and `admin` holds the other two. `meetings:access` also opens the user directory for the participant picker (ADR 20261104-user-directory-gate-meetings-access). New service client `meetings-api` and scope `host-notices.send`.
  - **shell:** the gated tool at `/meetings` and the ungated "My meetings" page at `/my-meetings`, so a participant without tool access can still read what was captured about them. Adds the host-extension seam that lets another remote embed a Meetings panel, and the meetings-api origin for the session.
  - **Operators:** `meetings-api` needs its own database, an EU bucket for audio and the env contract in `tools/env-doctor`.
- c30a110: Meetings no longer trusts a transcript to be complete, and says so when it may not be (specs/20261104-meetings A7, plan amendment 2). Long recordings are transcribed in overlapping parts that are stitched back together; each part is checked for completeness (gaps, early end), and a part with probable gaps marks the transcript possibly incomplete, with the gaps shown where they are. A part is not retried: the provider returns the same result for the same audio (specs/20261104-meetings A8). Speaker labels from different parts are joined by text only, never by audio, and a "Separate by part" action splits a joined speaker back into one per part (names are copied across). Summaries and answers written from a possibly incomplete transcript carry a service-written caveat that the model cannot drop or alter. Claude on Vertex now runs with one unforced tool, and the evals gain channels for completeness and caveats.
  - **meetings-api:** the transcript endpoint returns part ends, segment parts, gaps, the default speaker index and the possibly-incomplete notice; new `POST transcript/speakers/:key/separate`; a summary keeps a snapshot of the gaps it was written with.
  - **meetings-ui:** shows the possibly-incomplete notice and the caveats on summaries and answers, and offers "Separate by part" on joined speakers.

### Patch Changes

- 1f09e79: Long recordings no longer fail to transcribe: each 30-minute part is now streamed from Gemini on Vertex AI (`streamGenerateContent`), so a reply that takes more than five minutes is no longer lost on the way back, and at most two parts run at once, which keeps Vertex's shared capacity from refusing them with 429. Every request now logs one content-free line (part, outcome, HTTP status, finish reason, duration, bytes), so the cause of a failed transcription is visible.
- 50fac62: Where two parts of a long recording overlap, the same speech no longer appears twice under two speaker names when the two parts heard a few words differently: the overlap is aligned word by word (near-equal and differently heard words pair up when their times are close) instead of needing 8 identical words in a row. Speech found in only one part is still kept.
- 3055c7e: Long recordings no longer split the same person into a new speaker label at every part: a label is joined to the previous part's when at least 80 % of the words both parts transcribed agree in both directions (it needed 100 %), and a silence where the parts overlap no longer prevents it. Refusals are logged by cause.
- c75b9eb: Tests only: the env-group test no longer picks up a local dotenv file, and the ogg probe fixture uses ffmpeg's built-in Vorbis encoder instead of the optional libvorbis.
