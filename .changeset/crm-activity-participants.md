CRM activities name the colleagues who took part (specs/20261101-crm US-8). A call, a meeting, a note or a task can list up to 10 participants with CRM access, for example "Meeting at the client, with Paro". The activity shows them, a task appears in the task list of everyone taking part, and a colleague added by someone else is notified in-app. A task still has one assignee, who answers for it.
