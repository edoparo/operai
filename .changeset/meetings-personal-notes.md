Personal meeting notes: everyone who can open a meeting has their own notes on it, private to them (not the meeting's creator, not the AI, not the logs), written in a WYSIWYG editor (bold, italic, a heading, bulleted, numbered and check lists) and saved by themselves a moment after typing stops, when leaving the editor or the page; a save from a stale tab asks to reload instead of overwriting. The design system gains `RichTextEditor` in its own entry, `@operai/ui/rich-text` (TipTap, installed only by the apps that use it), and six formatting icons.
