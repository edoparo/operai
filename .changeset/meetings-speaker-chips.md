Transcript speakers are easier to sort out when the transcription found more voices than people: labels assigned to the same participant (or renamed alike) show as one chip, "from N labels"; a chip shows only that person's lines, for everyone who can read the transcript; Listen plays the person's longest line and stops at its end, to recognise the voice; renaming a merged chip renames every label in it.
