Meetings get an agenda: an ordered list of points (a title and an optional note, up to 30) that the creator and the Operai participants with Meetings access write, reorder and tick as covered, also from the recorder widget during a recording; everyone who can open the meeting reads it. A summary can follow it: the new built-in template "By agenda" has one section per point plus "Other topics", and any other template can "Follow the agenda". The agenda is quoted data to the AI, never instructions, and a summary keeps the agenda it was made from.
