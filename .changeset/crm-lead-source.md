CRM companies and deals record where they came from (specs/017 US-7): direct inbound, direct outbound, event, referral or other. For an event, a referral or other, a detail can name which event or who referred it. A new deal starts from its company's source, and both pages show it, for example "Event · Swiss Digital Day 2026".
