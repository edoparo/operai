The meeting's Summaries, Transcript and Ask tabs are laid out as cards under a ruled tab bar, instead of bare content pressed against the tabs. The transcript has one tools card (audio, search, copy, download at the field's height) and one card for the text, with speakers as a labelled row, times in a fixed column and a speaker's name shown once per run of lines (still read out on every line). Ask keeps the conversation and the question box in one card, with questions shown on the right and answers on the left.
