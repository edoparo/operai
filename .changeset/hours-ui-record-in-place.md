My hours gains a Week view and hours-ui gains a Record hours button other tools can embed (specs/20261116-record-hours-in-place, ADR 20261116-week-view-sums-a-complete-read-in-the-browser, ADR 20261116-record-hours-widget-through-the-host-extension-seam). A List | Week switch at the top of My hours opens `/hours?view=week`: one week, Monday to Sunday, as a table with a row for each project, deal, meeting and kind of other work you recorded on, a column for each day, and the totals of every row, every day and the week, with Previous, Next and This week to move between weeks and the same category, work type, Recorded on, project and deal filters as the List. An empty cell of today or an earlier day records hours there with the day and what they were on already set; a cell with hours opens its entries with the list's Edit and Delete and an Add hours button; every figure follows a change without reloading. The figures are added up in the browser from one complete read of the week, so a page that fails shows an error and never a partial table. The remote also exposes `./RecordHours`: a button that opens the same entry dialog with the project, the deal or the task fixed and today as the day, records through the same API and rules as My hours, and says what it recorded with a link to My hours. Copy is English and Italian.
