CRM lists can be sorted and filtered (specs/20261101-crm US-9). Companies sort by name, status, entity, industry or city, and contacts by name, company, job title or email, by clicking the column header. The order is applied by the server, so it holds across every page. Companies also gain an owner filter: anyone, "mine", or one of the people who own a company.
