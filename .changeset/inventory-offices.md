Inventory: an asset can be held by an office (CH - Agno, CH - Lugano, YoRoom - Milano) instead of a person. Assigning to an office is immediate and works for any office whatever the asset's entity; return, lost and dispose clear it like a person. Offices are managed on a new Offices page (add, rename, archive); the asset list filters by office, and the holder column, detail, fiscal-year table, export and history show the office.
