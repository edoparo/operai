While a meeting is being recorded you can move anywhere in Operai: a recorder widget stays visible above every page, with the elapsed time, live sound levels for the voice coming in (microphone) and going out (shared tab), Pause, Resume, Stop and a way back to the meeting. Drag it, or move it with the arrow keys, around the edge of the window. Leaving the meeting no longer asks for confirmation; closing or reloading the tab still does. New library component `FloatingPanel`.
