The isolated PDF check's low-severity security notes are closed (L-12 to L-15, I-22, I-23). The child no longer loads a dotenv file of the working directory (`--no-env-file`, a working directory of its own that is empty). The check's place is taken before a confirm reads the file's bytes, and one user holds at most two of the queue's six places. A kill for time is remembered for a minute instead of for the life of the process. SIGTERM and SIGINT kill the live children, and a child ends itself at its own deadline when the service is killed outright. Memory is polled every 20 ms on Linux.
