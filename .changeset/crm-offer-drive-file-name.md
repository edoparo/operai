Offer PDFs now download under the name wellD uses in Drive: `WellD_<entity>_<client>_<deal>_<reference>.pdf`, for example `WellD_CH_STUDIOV_FMS_00092026.pdf`, and `WellD_CH_STUDIOV_FMS_00272026r2.pdf` for the second revision of offer 0027/2026. The client part is the company's name without spaces, accents or symbols, in upper case; the deal part is the initials of the deal's title ("Fleet Management System" → FMS). Offers generated earlier download under the new name too; the documents themselves are unchanged.
