The look of the document title is now adjustable in Letterheads. The look section of the configurator has a new "Document title" group next to the three heading levels, with size (8 to 36 pt), bold and colour. It styles the title line of a document, such as the "CONTRATTO DI FORNITURA" line of a CRM offer, which is always set in the headings font. The default is the 20 pt bold black used until now, so a version saved earlier opens unchanged and prints exactly as it did; a change of the title is listed as a change of "Headings and document title" in the version history.
