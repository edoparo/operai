Offers can be laid out by the letterhead service (specs/20261116-letterhead-service), behind `LETTERHEAD_SERVICE_ENABLED` (only the literal `true` turns it on; it needs `LETTERHEAD_API_URL` and the existing service-client pair, scope `letterhead:render`). Off, the default, nothing changes. On: a letterhead client (`letterheadClient.ts`) and `draftToBlocks` turn an offer draft into content blocks (`offers/blocks.ts`, `contentSchema.ts`); the wizard's generate and the classic offer routes draw their pages through the service and record the letterhead version in the new nullable `deal_offer.letterheadVersion` column (migration `offer_letterhead_version`, no row touched); two preview routes (offer draft and template try-out) answer with the service's pages, capped per person, and are 404 with the switch off; a page-less deal PDF is `attachment_unreadable`; `GET /scope` answers `letterheadService`; the letterhead upload routes answer 410 `letterhead_upload_retired`, with the 410 title in the problem table. Two scripts ship with it: `retire-uploaded-letterheads` (renders before it deletes) and `check-letterhead-service`. An over-long section is refused by the preview as it is by generate. Contract tests run against the real service, and the shared entity-scope fixture runs on the TypeScript side.
