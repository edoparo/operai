After the accountant's first look at the two files. Journal (format 3): the Description has no square brackets, its parts are joined by ` - ` (`Cancelleria - MC26/001 - EUR 10.40 = CHF 9.62 - Matteo Codogno`). Register extract (format 2): Period is the competence month being booked, the same on every row, not the month of each expense; by default the month before the batch's cutoff (Europe/Zurich), and the card offers a "Competence month" choice (`?period=YYYY-MM`). The sheet is named after that month's year.
