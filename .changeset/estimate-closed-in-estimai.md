A technical estimate is finished when it is closed in EstimAI. The owner or an editor closes it from the estimate page, after which it is read-only until someone reopens it. The CRM follows on its own: the deal's estimate turns ready, its task is completed, and the requester and the deal owner are told; reopening it in EstimAI reverses that. The CRM no longer has its own "Mark ready" and "Reopen" buttons. Needs `estimate-notices.send` on estimai-api's service client in auth, `CRM_INTERNAL_URL` on estimai-api and `SERVICE_AUDIENCE` on crm-api.
